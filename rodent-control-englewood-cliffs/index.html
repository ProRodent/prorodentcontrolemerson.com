Small precautions before the visit

The goal of these steps is to keep rodent dust from rising until help is on the way.

  • Dust is the hazard, so the plan is simple: wet it down before anything gets lifted.
  • The airing period comes first and cannot be skipped, even when the job looks small.
  • Gloves of rubber, latex or vinyl go on before your hands go near droppings, urine marks or nest stuffing.
  • Contact time does the work: spray generously, pause, and wipe only after the liquid has had its turn.
  • After the wiping is finished, mop or sponge the floor and counters with disinfectant too.
  • Stay alert to your own health for a month or so, and let a doctor evaluate any flu-like symptoms.
