Before the visit.

  • Notice whether a smell grows stronger when the furnace or air conditioning runs.
  • Wipe down food prep areas and put crumbs and open boxes away.
  • Never reach into a wall opening, a vent or a pile of stored boxes.
  • Keep a list of rooms where you have seen pellets.

Small precautions before the visit

This is a summary of federal pages, not medical advice. For questions about your own health, ask your doctor.

  • Resist the urge to tidy quickly with a broom, because speed and dust arrive together.
  • A room that has sat sealed for weeks deserves a cross-breeze, so prop two openings and walk away for a while.
  • Bare skin should never meet rodent waste, even for a moment.
  • Wetting everything down keeps dust grounded and gives the disinfectant time to act.
  • Collect all of it in a single sealed bag and carry that bag out of the building.
  • Questions about a symptom belong with a medical professional, who can decide if rodents are involved.
