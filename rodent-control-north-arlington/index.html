How weather pushes animals.

Late winter is when sound travels best through a quiet house. Many callers first notice scratching in February, though the animals may have arrived earlier.

Cleaning in a safe order

Rodent waste may carry disease, which is why cleanup has to be damp and unhurried. The agencies listed at the foot of this page back up each line below.

  • Sweeping is the habit to drop first: dried droppings crumble, and the powder is easy to inhale.
  • Open each outside door and window first, then give the room about thirty minutes.
  • Keep your hands covered for the entire job and wash them with soap and water when the gloves come off.
  • Contact time does the work: spray generously, pause, and wipe only after the liquid has had its turn.
  • Use tongs or a gloved hand to lift a carcass, and seal it in a bag at once.
  • When you see a doctor about any illness, mention the cleanup, since the clinician needs that detail.
