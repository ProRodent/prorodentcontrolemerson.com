What health agencies advise

The goal of these steps is to keep rodent dust from rising until help is on the way.

  • Spritz dried waste with liquid first and only then touch it with a towel.
  • CDC guidance asks you to ventilate a closed area before cleaning, and a half hour is the figure it gives.
  • Single-use gloves suit this job as long as you wash well after taking them off.
  • Disinfectant needs a few minutes on the mess, so apply it and step back.
  • Tie the bag before you carry it anywhere, then take it straight outside.
  • After exposure, a cough, a fever or short breath is reason enough to ring a doctor soon.
