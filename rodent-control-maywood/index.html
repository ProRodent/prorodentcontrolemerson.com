Cleaning in a safe order

Nothing here requires calling us first. A county health office would give you much the same directions for pellets found indoors.

  • Keep every dry cleaning tool out of the affected area, the shop vacuum included.
  • A room that has sat sealed for weeks deserves a cross-breeze, so prop two openings and walk away for a while.
  • Anything that mice or rats have fouled counts as contaminated, so wear hand protection and wash afterward.
  • Disinfectant needs a few minutes on the mess, so apply it and step back.
  • Double-bag anything wet, dripping or sharp before it goes in the bin.
  • A fever, sore muscles or labored breathing that shows up after a cleanup means a doctor visit, with the rodent contact mentioned. Ours is a pest business, not a clinic.
