Who governs Haledon, and why it matters little.

Haledon became a borough in Passaic County in 1908, as far as our sources show. It is roughly 8.3 miles from Emerson, and older homes in a place like this often have more gaps for mice to use.

Reading the seasons.

Spring brings its own surge. Animals that wintered in a wall cavity breed, and sounds that were faint in January become louder as litters grow.

Heavy rain can send burrowing animals uphill and toward foundations. A wet week can start activity that looked unlikely a month earlier.

From first look to final check.

The materials are chosen to resist teeth: steel mesh, sheet metal and a sealant that hardens. Foam or caulk alone will not last.

A return visit counts what was caught, tests each repair and decides whether any trap should move. If the count is zero and no new signs appear, the job moves to its last stage.

Because every property has its own history, the technician treats the first walk around the outside as the real start of the job, noticing vents, pipe collars, door seals and plantings before going indoors.

The phone line is the only way to book, and that is deliberate, since a conversation with a person lets the dispatcher ask about your street, your building and your timing before anyone is sent out.

We would rather explain a small problem accurately than sell a large solution, so if the walk-through finds only a single entry point and a single animal, the written plan will say so in plain words.

Cleaning in a safe order

Come back to this box each time fresh droppings, stains or chewed bedding show up.

  • Dust is the hazard, so the plan is simple: wet it down before anything gets lifted.
  • Time the airing with your phone: thirty minutes with doors and windows open, nobody working in the space.
  • Gloves go on before you start and stay on until the last bag is tied.
  • Wet the waste thoroughly, wait the five minutes, then pick it up with paper towels.
  • Wipe tools, mop heads and tabletop surfaces that touched the mess with the same disinfectant.
  • After exposure, a cough, a fever or short breath is reason enough to ring a doctor soon.
