How weather pushes animals.

Leaf drifts against a foundation collect in October and November, and they hide gaps from view. Raking them back is one of the cheapest preventive tasks of the year.

Summer storms can knock branches onto roofs, and a branch that touches the house is a bridge. Check the roofline after a major storm.

Small precautions before the visit

This is a summary of federal pages, not medical advice. For questions about your own health, ask your doctor.

  • Sweeping is the habit to drop first: dried droppings crumble, and the powder is easy to inhale.
  • Time the airing with your phone: thirty minutes with doors and windows open, nobody working in the space.
  • Gloves go on before you start and stay on until the last bag is tied.
  • The surface should look drenched rather than damp before you start wiping.
  • Tie the bag before you carry it anywhere, then take it straight outside.
  • A fever, sore muscles or labored breathing that shows up after a cleanup means a doctor visit, with the rodent contact mentioned. Ours is a pest business, not a clinic.
