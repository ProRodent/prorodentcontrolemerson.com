What health agencies advise

Treat this as a short guide to the first hour after you notice droppings.

  • Keep every dry cleaning tool out of the affected area, the shop vacuum included.
  • CDC guidance asks you to ventilate a closed area before cleaning, and a half hour is the figure it gives.
  • Keep a gloved hand away from your face while working, then wash both hands well.
  • A generous soak, a short wait and a towel wipe, in that order, is the pattern the CDC lays out.
  • Use tongs or a gloved hand to lift a carcass, and seal it in a bag at once.
  • We do not diagnose illness. Anyone who feels sick after exposure should see a clinician.
