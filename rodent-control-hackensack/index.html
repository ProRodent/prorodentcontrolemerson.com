What health agencies advise

Come back to this box each time fresh droppings, stains or chewed bedding show up.

  • Dry rags raise the same cloud a broom does, so keep them for other chores.
  • The airing period comes first and cannot be skipped, even when the job looks small.
  • Anything that mice or rats have fouled counts as contaminated, so wear hand protection and wash afterward.
