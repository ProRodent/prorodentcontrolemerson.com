From first look to final check.

The technician begins outside and circles the house unhurriedly, studying eaves, vents, pipe collars, the footing and the garage door. Each gap and each trace of travel gets noted.

A written estimate is handed over after the survey rather than quoted over the phone, and it separates trapping, sealing, cleanout and repair so that you always know exactly which task each figure pays for.
