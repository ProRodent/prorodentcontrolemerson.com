Cleaning in a safe order

Come back to this box each time fresh droppings, stains or chewed bedding show up.

  • Spritz dried waste with liquid first and only then touch it with a towel.
  • Open each outside door and window first, then give the room about thirty minutes.
  • Gloves of rubber, latex or vinyl go on before your hands go near droppings, urine marks or nest stuffing.
  • The surface should look drenched rather than damp before you start wiping.
  • Used towels, droppings and any dead rodent all go into one plastic bag, which you knot and put out with the weekly trash.
  • Stay alert to your own health for a month or so, and let a doctor evaluate any flu-like symptoms.
