How the stages link up.

Most jobs cross several pages. A frequent order runs survey, trapping, sealing, return visit, then cleanout and repairs, because new insulation laid over an old nest is simply fouled all over again.

Every stage comes with its own quote, so trapping and sealing can go ahead now while cleanout or repair waits for a better moment.

What belongs to someone else.

No page exists for urgent cases, because the same phone number reaches the earliest slot. Reroofing, medical questions and general carpentry fall outside our work, and the dispatcher points you to the right trade if those come up.

Service page questions.

Must I book more than one stage?

No, each service is complete on its own. If neighboring work would help, the technician says so.

Do you handle raccoons or squirrels?

Our work covers mice and rats. Should the survey point to some other animal, we tell you at the property and direct you elsewhere.

Where should a first-time caller begin?

Normally with the survey, because it names the species and shows its way in. Sealing waits for trapping, so no animal ends up shut inside a wall.

Do shops and offices use these pages too?

They do. Businesses have a page of their own, built around repeat rounds that each finish with a written note.
