A calendar of rodent pressure.

Leaf drifts against a foundation collect in October and November, and they hide gaps from view. Raking them back is one of the cheapest preventive tasks of the year.

Chilly nights set things off. When the thermometer sinks, mice and rats start probing houses for warmth, and the first noises often follow within days.
