Cleaning in a safe order

Patient wet work stops particles from getting into your lungs.

  • Spritz dried waste with liquid first and only then touch it with a towel.
  • Let outdoor air flow through the space for a half hour, then come back with gloves and spray.
  • Single-use gloves suit this job as long as you wash well after taking them off.
  • Wet the waste thoroughly, wait the five minutes, then pick it up with paper towels.
  • Once the bag has gone in the bin, wash your hands again, even with clean gloves on.
  • A fever, sore muscles or labored breathing that shows up after a cleanup means a doctor visit, with the rodent contact mentioned. Ours is a pest business, not a clinic.
