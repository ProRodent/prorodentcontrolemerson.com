Purpose of this website.

Service pages and guides here explain rodents and our methods in general terms. Treat them as background reading rather than medical, legal or building-code guidance, and rely on a site visit for anything specific to your property.

Estimates and arrival windows.

Once the walk-through ends, a written price lists every task separately: sealing, waste removal, damage repair. Arrival depends on the schedule and on traffic, so the dispatcher names a window over the phone. Only what is said on that call binds us.

No guaranteed outcomes.

Rodent activity responds to factors we do not control, from a neighbor's yard to nearby plantings and the weather. A visit delivers the work described to you, but no company can promise that animals will never return.

Safety and health notes.

Follow the safety notes on these pages and on the CDC and EPA pages we link. If you start to feel sick following possible contact with droppings, consult a doctor.
