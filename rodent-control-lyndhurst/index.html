How a technician works.

Sealing waits until the catch count falls. Closing a hole too soon can shut animals inside and send them chewing through a wall.

The materials are chosen to resist teeth: steel mesh, sheet metal and a sealant that hardens. Foam or caulk alone will not last.

Weather, months and rodent activity.

Fall is the season to finish sealing. Gaps closed in September are far cheaper than infestations treated in January.

Heavy rain can send burrowing animals uphill and toward foundations. A wet week can start activity that looked unlikely a month earlier.

Cleaning in a safe order

Come back to this box each time fresh droppings, stains or chewed bedding show up.

  • Dry rags raise the same cloud a broom does, so keep them for other chores.
  • Sheds, cabins and garages that were shut for a long time want half an hour of fresh air before anyone works inside.
  • Gloves of rubber, latex or vinyl go on before your hands go near droppings, urine marks or nest stuffing.
  • Wet the waste thoroughly, wait the five minutes, then pick it up with paper towels.
