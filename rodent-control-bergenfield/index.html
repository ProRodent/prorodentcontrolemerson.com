A short profile of Bergenfield.

What prompts a Bergenfield call.

The signs below are what usually prompt a call about Bergenfield:

  • Holes chewed in a vent screen or the garage door seal.
  • Grease marks along baseboards or pipes.
  • Patter inside a wall or above a ceiling once the lights go out.
  • Tracks in dust on a basement or attic floor.

What helps us plan the visit.

A quick description of the building helps more than people expect. For a Bergenfield address, think about the following.

  • If you rent, whether the owner or manager controls the basement and trash area.

The shape of the job.

The technician begins outside and circles the house unhurriedly, studying eaves, vents, pipe collars, the footing and the garage door. Each gap and each trace of travel gets noted.

Traps go on the routes the evidence shows, not in random corners. Each device is recorded, and the plan states when it will be checked.

Nothing about the work depends on guessing, because a technician reads the evidence in your building, follows it to the openings that animals use, and writes down both the findings and the plan so that nothing is left to memory.

A written estimate is handed over after the survey rather than quoted over the phone, and it separates trapping, sealing, cleanout and repair so that you always know exactly which task each figure pays for.

People who live near Bergenfield sometimes assume that a clean kitchen is enough to keep rodents away, but animals also come for shelter and warmth, so the condition of the foundation and roofline counts at least as much as housekeeping.

Before touching any pellet

The goal of these steps is to keep rodent dust from rising until help is on the way.

  • Keep every dry cleaning tool out of the affected area, the shop vacuum included.
  • Open each outside door and window first, then give the room about thirty minutes.
  • Gloves of rubber, latex or vinyl go on before your hands go near droppings, urine marks or nest stuffing.
  • Wet the waste thoroughly, wait the five minutes, then pick it up with paper towels.
  • Once the bag has gone in the bin, wash your hands again, even with clean gloves on.
  • Our expertise ends at the pest side, so anything about your body goes to a physician.
