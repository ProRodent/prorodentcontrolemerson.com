Most conversations with Garfield callers start from something small, such as:

When animals move.

Heavy rain can send burrowing animals uphill and toward foundations. A wet week can start activity that looked unlikely a month earlier.

Before touching any pellet

This is a summary of federal pages, not medical advice. For questions about your own health, ask your doctor.

  • Resist the urge to tidy quickly with a broom, because speed and dust arrive together.
  • Open each outside door and window first, then give the room about thirty minutes.
  • Gloves of rubber, latex or vinyl go on before your hands go near droppings, urine marks or nest stuffing.
  • Wet the waste thoroughly, wait the five minutes, then pick it up with paper towels.
  • Collect all of it in a single sealed bag and carry that bag out of the building.
  • Questions about a symptom belong with a medical professional, who can decide if rodents are involved.
