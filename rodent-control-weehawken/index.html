Dust and droppings: a safer way

Spending a few minutes preparing before you touch anything costs little and protects your breathing.

  • Resist the urge to tidy quickly with a broom, because speed and dust arrive together.
  • Sheds, cabins and garages that were shut for a long time want half an hour of fresh air before anyone works inside.
  • Hand protection comes first, and a soapy wash comes last.
  • Disinfectant needs a few minutes on the mess, so apply it and step back.
  • Use tongs or a gloved hand to lift a carcass, and seal it in a bag at once.
